The Enclosure

The phone app a corporate client's guests use on their race day: the card, their picks in the Corporate Cup, the feed of results, notes and photos, and the host's Steward's Room. These pages are built from the Django site by tools/build-enclosure.py: a real card (Bath, Tuesday 29 September 2026) and a made-up room, Hartwell & Penn's eleven guests. Start with docs/enclosure-brief.md.

It is a phone app: look at it at phone width (375px) first. In Chrome, DevTools' device toolbar (Ctrl+Shift+M) does it.
