The conviction sits at medium — the market move and the Teal yard form are strong signals, but Legendsoftheland's class drop means this is a genuine two-horse race rather than a foregone conclusion. On Mr Fox's pick: The Thames Boatman is a horse I read carefully. The SR of 78 actually leads this field, and the class drop of two rungs is a legitimate positive driver. But The Thames Boatman is drifting — from a 9/2 recent SP to 9/1 today — carrying 13lb more than last time, and Richard Hughes's yard is at 17% without a specific positive signal for this runner. The SR advantage is real, but a horse the market is running away from under that kind of weight burden is not where I want to be when Distant Rumble is being backed with conviction and the trainer is operating in one of his hottest spells. Mr Fox's read on the class drop is sound; the drift and the weight load are the reasons I don't follow him here.
