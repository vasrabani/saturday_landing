    SR 80 is the class of the field and form string is genuinely improving (-84321), but the market drift from favourite to 7/2 is a concrete red flag I cannot dismiss.

Mr Fox has Decalogue, and I understand the case — SR 80 is the best number in the race, the form string is a clean improvement arc, and Jamie Osborne has trained winners. But Decalogue was 11/4 favourite last time out and is being let go at 7/2 today. That is not noise; that is the market revising its opinion with information I should not ignore. The specific data point that ends my interest in Decalogue is that drift combined with Osborne's 14-day yard running at 7% — the yard is not in form. I back Dryburgh each-way at 7/1: a steaming market, a hot yard at 20%, distance and going both positive, and a model score within a rounding error of the top-ranked horse at more than twice the price.
