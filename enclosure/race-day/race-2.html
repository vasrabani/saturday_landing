Hebridean Nomad is my bet. The steamer signal, the top deterministic model score, the positive distance fit, and Balding's yard ticking over — these are four independent signals pointing the same direction. Win, 20 units at 13/2. Conviction band: medium. A credible danger exists in Moab, whose 24lb weight drop is not something to dismiss lightly, and Society Girl's front-running position with the highest SR in the field means she must always be feared. On Mr Fox's tip — Two Plus Two is 15/2 and drifting, sits in a hold-up position that suits the pace map, and the SR of 68 is workmanlike. But the model has Two Plus Two sixth of six scored runners, the drifter flag is a negative signal, and Ed de Giles' yard is blank over 14 days. Mr Fox and I split here: the market is moving away from his pick, not toward it, and Hebridean Nomad is the stronger evidence-based call.
