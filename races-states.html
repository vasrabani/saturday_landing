Races page states

Every state the day's races page can render, so each one can be designed rather than imagined. Generated from races.html, races-tomorrow.html and races-yesterday.html by node tools/build-races-states.mjs, which drives the real pages: the revealed winners, Reveal all, the reveal track and the marked tab are captured from them, not written here. The template's branches that none of the three days caught are marked a stand-in: their markup is the smallest change to a captured page that gives the template's own class names (tests/support/races-branches.cjs), to be checked against meetings_browse.html in the port. A case that depends on the width of the window (the phone's, the tablet's) is shown in a window of its own.
